# Product context

People read the same book and lose the thread of who is where. Group chats are noisy. Spreadsheets are sad.

PageMate is a quiet companion: one active book, two progress bars, a lead sentence (“you are 12 pages ahead”), and a tiny activity feed. You share **that book** with your buddy — not your whole account. Reactions and spoiler-free notes attach to a page number so you can cheer without ruining chapter 14.

The product should feel like a native utility: Add to Home Screen, works in a subway tunnel (queue the page turn), and pings you when your person moves.
