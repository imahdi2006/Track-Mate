# Product context

People read the same book and lose the thread of who is where. Group chats are noisy. Spreadsheets are sad.

Trackmate is a quiet companion: a reading **room** (2–5 people), a shared shelf of books, courses, and movies, progress per person, and a tiny activity feed. You share **a book** — they see that title, not your whole library or account. Room invites exist but warn that they share the whole shelf. Reactions and spoiler-free notes attach to a page number and show who wrote them, when, and who can see them.

The product should feel like a native utility: Add to Home Screen on HTTPS, works offline for page turns (queue), and pings roommates when someone moves.
