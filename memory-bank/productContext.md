# Product context

People read the same book and lose the thread of who is where. Group chats are noisy. Spreadsheets are sad.

BookMate is a quiet companion: a reading **room** (2–5 people), a shared shelf, progress per reader, and a tiny activity feed. You share **a book** — not your password. Room invites exist but warn that they share the whole shelf. Reactions and spoiler-free notes attach to a page number.

The product should feel like a native utility: Add to Home Screen on HTTPS, works offline for page turns (queue), and pings roommates when someone moves.
